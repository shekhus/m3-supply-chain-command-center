## What the numbers do not say

- Precision counts every HIGH event with no seeded anomaly behind it as wrong. Some of those are genuine movements in a random world — the generator seeds six anomalies but does not promise the other 529 days are flat. The number is therefore a floor, not an estimate of real-world precision.
- Recall is measured at WARN and above; precision at HIGH. An event that stayed at WARN was recorded and never interrupted anyone.
- Attribution is scored only where there was something coarser to decompose. Anomalies detected at the grain they were seeded at name their own segment, and are not counted either way.
